# SKILLS

The skill catalog for this Snowball. One entry per skill: what it does, when to invoke it, what to listen for in my messages.

> Auto-regenerated by `/end-session`. Read this before responding to my first message in any session.

Status legend: `[live]` = built and ready. `[not yet built]` = placeholder; the skill is on the roadmap but not on disk yet.

---

## `/day-one` — first-time setup walkthrough

**Purpose.** Walks me through Day One of my Snowball: voice tool, two settings (bypass mode + model picker), website + LinkedIn scrape, personalized identity paragraph. Ends with a soft-ask handoff to `/skill-builder`. One-shot per client; assumes a guided session.

**Invoke when I say:**
- "set me up"
- "first time"
- "I just opened this"
- "walk me through Day One"
- or you spot a `SNOWBALL_BUNDLE` marker in my first message (paste from king-intelligence.com/levelup)

## `/skill-builder` — build a new skill for a recurring task

**Status:** `[live]`

**Purpose.** The compositional engine that grows new skills. Routes to `/grill-me` when I have a task in mind, or `/brainstorm` when I don't. Captures how long the task takes me by hand, blocks on missing connections or missing voice profile, generates the skill, tests it once with a real record, and updates this catalog.

**Invoke when I say:**
- "build me a skill"
- "automate this"
- "I keep doing X by hand"
- "I want a skill for Y"
- "what should I automate next"
- or paste a recurring task with any "turn this into a skill" framing

## `/begin-session` — load a topic and brief me

**Status:** `[live]`

**Purpose.** Topic-loader. Resolves a named client / project / initiative to its folder under `snowball/clients/` or `snowball/projects/`, fans out in parallel across the folder + auto-memory + wired connectors (Gmail, Calendar, HubSpot, Otter, Notion, Drive), and hands back a tight chat-only briefing. Empty sections get omitted entirely (no filler). Bare invocation surfaces a recent-folders menu.

**Invoke when I say:**
- "load up Atlas Brewery for me"
- "get me up to speed on Q4 Pricing"
- "where are we with <client>"
- "/begin-session" (with or without a topic)
- or I open the session by deeply engaging a specific named topic

**Do NOT invoke** for quick tasks or general questions — those don't need background context.

## `/end-session` — close the session

**Status:** `[not yet built]`

**Purpose.** Detects what changed this session, regenerates `CONNECTIONS.md` and `SKILLS.md` from live truth, recomputes the `TIME-SAVED.md` total, writes a briefing for next time, runs a single memory-pruning confirmation, then commits and pushes to my private GitHub backup.

**Invoke when I say:**
- "wrapping up"
- "done for today"
- "stopping"
- "calling it"

## `/grill-me` — stress-test a plan I already have

**Status:** `[not yet built]`

**Purpose.** Interviews me relentlessly about a plan or design until every branch of the decision tree is resolved. The output is a tight spec I (or `/skill-builder`) can execute against.

**Invoke when I say:**
- "grill me"
- "poke holes in this"
- "stress-test this plan"
- "I have a plan, push back on it"

**Use when there's a plan or design to stress-test. For open exploration with no plan yet, use `/brainstorm` instead.**

## `/brainstorm` — explore openly when I don't have a plan yet

**Status:** `[live]`

**Purpose.** Open-ended exploration partner. Helps me figure out what to build, automate, or change next when I don't yet know the answer.

**Invoke when I say:**
- "help me think"
- "brainstorm with me"
- "I don't know where to start"
- "what should I do about X"

**Use for open exploration. Once a direction emerges, route me to `/grill-me` to stress-test it before building.**
