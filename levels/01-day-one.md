# Level 1 — Day One

Welcome. This is your first session. By the end of today, you'll have:

- Your business written into Claude (so it stops being generic)
- A list of every task you do regularly
- Your Day 0 scorecard
- One small task already moved off your plate

About 90 minutes. Take it slow.

---

## Step 1 — Tell Claude who you are (~15 min)

Open `about-me.md` and `about-business.md`. Fill in what you can. Don't worry about being perfect. Skip anything you don't know — we'll come back to it in Level 2.

When you're done, say to Claude:

> *I just filled in about-me and about-business. Read them and tell me what's missing or unclear.*

Claude will read them and ask you a few follow-up questions.

---

## Step 2 — Run the inventory skill (~20 min)

Type into Claude:

> /what-do-i-do

This is a skill that interviews you about your work and turns it into a task list. It walks you through 7 buckets:

1. **Revenue** — where money comes in
2. **Customers** — who buys from you
3. **Calendar** — where your time goes
4. **Communication** — where messages live
5. **Tasks** — what you do over and over
6. **Meetings** — your calls, prep, notes
7. **Knowledge** — where your know-how lives

Just answer Claude's questions. The output goes into `tasks.md`.

---

## Step 3 — Score yourself (Day 0) (~5 min)

Open `intake.md`. Answer the three questions honestly.

Save the file. We'll re-ask these same three questions on Day 42 (end of Level 6) and look at the change.

---

## Step 4 — Your first tiny win (~30 min)

Look at `tasks.md`. Pick ONE task. Make it small. Something you do almost every day.

Say to Claude:

> *Help me do [task name] right now. I want to see how much of this you can do for me.*

Watch what Claude does. Ask questions when you're confused. (*"Explain like I'm an idiot"* is the magic phrase.)

When you're done:
- If Claude did most of it: mark that task in `tasks.md` as **Score: 2**
- If Claude did the whole thing: mark it **Score: 3**

---

## Step 5 — One mindset shift to sit with (~5 min)

Before you close this session, read this twice:

> **Anything that can be done with a mouse and a keyboard can be done by AI. The definition of work is changing.**

That's the shift we're making. You're not a person who does mouse-and-keyboard work anymore. You're the person who points AI at it.

Between now and Level 2, every time you're about to do something on your computer, ask yourself: *"Could AI do this — or at least 30% of this?"*

Just notice. Don't try to automate everything yet. Just notice.

---

## You're done with Level 1. What you have now:

- ✅ A `CLAUDE.md` that knows the basics about you
- ✅ `about-me.md` + `about-business.md` filled in
- ✅ `tasks.md` — every recurring task in your business, scored
- ✅ `intake.md` — your Day 0 baseline
- ✅ One task already moved off your plate
- ✅ The default-shift habit

## Between now and Level 2

- Use Claude every day for at least one task. Try to move another task to Score 2.
- Keep `tasks.md` open. Add tasks you forgot. Edit scores as they change.
- Practice the default-shift question 5 times.

When you're ready, open `levels/02-context.md`.
