# Level 4 — Connect Your Tools

*Available after Level 3.*

We plug Claude into the actual tools you live in — Gmail, Calendar, Drive, your CRM, your scheduler. The system stops being a chat box and starts reaching into your world.

What you'll get:
- Google Workspace connected (Gmail / Calendar / Drive / Docs / Sheets in one shot)
- 1-2 of your other tools connected (CRM, scheduler, etc.)
- Claude can use a browser to do things on the web for you
- One workflow that crosses multiple tools (look up someone in CRM → write email → add calendar invite)
