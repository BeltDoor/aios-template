# Level 6 — Run Forever (Graduation)

*Available after Level 5. The final session.*

This is the level where things start running on their own. We schedule recipes to run every morning, every week, every month — without you. And we score the program.

What you'll get:
- A morning brief that runs every day at 8am — pulls from your calendar, email, recent activity
- 1+ scheduled workflow that runs on its own
- Day 42 retest of `intake.md` — score the deltas
- The Showdown — Claude takes a 10-question test about your business; if it scores 7+/10 with at least 3 "better than me" answers, your assistant is up to speed
- Final tally on `tasks.md` — what % of your tasks are at Score 2 or 3 (target 60-70%)
- Your extension playbook — what to do when a new tool comes out, when something breaks, when you want to teach someone else
