# Your AI Operating System

Welcome. This folder is yours. It's the start of your AI Operating System — the place where you teach AI everything about you, your business, and how you work, so it can start handling more and more of your day.

## How the game works

You play this in levels. Each level is small, has a clear goal, and ends with a small win.

- **Level 1 — Day One:** set up your business in here, run a task inventory, get your first tiny win. ← *start here*
- **Level 2 — Context:** teach Claude how you talk and how your business works.
- **Level 3 — First Workflow:** build one real workflow from your actual life.
- **Level 4 — Connect Your Tools:** plug Claude into your calendar, email, CRM.
- **Level 5 — Build Something:** make a small thing for your business.
- **Level 6 — Run Forever:** schedule things to run on their own. Graduation.

**Open `levels/01-day-one.md` to start.**

## What's in this folder

- `CLAUDE.md` — what Claude reads about your business every time. You'll fill this in.
- `about-me.md` — who you are.
- `about-business.md` — what your business does.
- `tasks.md` — every recurring task in your business (filled in during Level 1).
- `intake.md` — your Day 0 score (filled in during Level 1, re-scored on Day 42).
- `levels/` — instructions for each level.
- `voice/` — examples of how you talk. Filled in during Level 2.
- `clients/`, `projects/`, `recipes/` — folders that fill up as you go.

## How long this takes

About 90 minutes per level, once a week, for 6 weeks. You play one level. You live with it for a week. Then you play the next one.

## When you get stuck

Type into Claude: *"Explain like I'm an idiot."* That's the magic phrase.

If Claude is doing something you don't understand, that's the line. Use it.
