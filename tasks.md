# My Tasks (Day-1 Inventory)

This is filled in by Claude during Module 02 (Intake) — every recurring task in your business, with a rough weekly-hours estimate on each. The hours are your Day-0 baseline. You re-score this near the end of the program; the hours taken off your plate are the proof it worked.

---

## How tasks are scored

Every task gets a score 0-3 based on how much AI is doing the work for you.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **0** — All me | I do this entirely by hand. AI plays no role. |
| **1** — AI helps a little | AI drafts, I rewrite a lot. Saves a little time. |
| **2** — AI does most | AI does most of it; I review and tweak. ← *counts as handled* |
| **3** — AI does it | AI does the task. I just check it (or it just runs on its own). ← *counts as handled* |

**Goal:** 60-70% of these tasks at Score 2 or 3 by the end of the program.
