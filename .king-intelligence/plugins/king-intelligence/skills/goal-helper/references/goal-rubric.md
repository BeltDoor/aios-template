# Stage 1 rubric: sharpen the goal

A loop can only finish if the goal names a finish line. Most goals a person says out loud are activities ("work on my website"), not outcomes ("the homepage headline and three section blurbs are rewritten and live"). Your job in this stage is to turn the activity into an outcome the loop can actually be done with.

## A good goal has four things

1. **A concrete outcome, not just an activity.** What's different in the world when this is finished? Name the thing that will exist or have changed.
2. **A target result.** The artifact or state that proves it's done: a page that's live, a list that's filled, a draft that's sent, a number that's hit.
3. **A scope line.** What's in, what's out, and how deep to go. This is what stops a loop from wandering into "while I'm here, let me also..."
4. **Context it must read first.** Which files, notes, or sources the worker has to look at before starting, so it works from fact instead of guessing. (Context usually lives in the relevant folder `CLAUDE.md` and in whatever notes you keep.)

## Questions that sharpen a fuzzy goal

- If two people looked at the result, what would make them agree it's done?
- Which words in the goal are subjective ("better", "cleaner", "modern")? Each one needs a concrete stand-in before Stage 2.
- What should the loop *not* touch?
- What does it need to read before it starts so it isn't inventing?

## Kill these (they can never be "done")

- "Improve the website": improve what, to what?
- "Make it good": by whose measure, checked how?
- "Research X": to support which decision? Research with no decision behind it loops forever.
- A goal that needs information the loop never gathers.
- A goal that's just "keep polishing" with no point where it stops.

## Before / after

**Weak:** "Work on my website."
**Sharp:** "Rewrite the homepage hero headline and the three service blurbs so they lead with the reader's benefit, not 'I build X'. Live on the site. Header nav, footer, and pricing left untouched. Read your brand and voice notes first."

**Weak:** "Clean up the CRM."
**Sharp:** "Every card on the CRM board has a person name, a one-line context note, and is in the right list. No card left blank or in the default inbox list. Don't create new cards, only fix existing ones."

**Weak:** "Get the testimonial sorted."
**Sharp:** "Cut the raw testimonial video to a tight vertical clip under 90 seconds, branded, and live on the testimonials page. The interviewer's voice is removed. Don't touch the other testimonials already on the page."

Once the goal names an outcome, a target result, a scope line, and the context to read, move to Stage 2 and turn each subjective word into a check.
