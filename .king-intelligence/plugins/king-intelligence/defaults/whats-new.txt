Claude can now work inside your Google Drive. Until now, if your business ran on Drive, Claude could only see whatever happened to be sitting on your laptop, which for a lot of people is almost nothing. Type /install-google and about three minutes later your whole Drive is a normal folder on this machine: Claude can read and edit every PDF, contract, spreadsheet and photo in it, and it finally shows up in your file map. If most of your writing lives in Google Docs rather than Word, the same command has a second, longer setup that opens those too.
