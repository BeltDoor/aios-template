# Where my work lives

_Not mapped yet._

This file is the map of where your real business files actually are: your Desktop, your Documents, and any OneDrive, SharePoint, iCloud Drive or Dropbox you have synced. Once it's filled in, every session reads it instead of asking you where things are.

**To fill it in, paste this into the chat:**

```
Read .claude/skills/map-my-work/SKILL.md and follow it to work out where my real work lives on this computer.
```

It asks your permission first. It reads folder and file **names only**, never opens a document, and **never moves, copies, or renames anything**. If your folders turn out to be a mess, it writes you a clean-up prompt you can run whenever you want, rather than tidying on its own.
